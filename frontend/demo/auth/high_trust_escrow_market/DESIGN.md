## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, institutional-grade commercial platform tailored for high-stakes brokered transactions, property settlements, and escrow services. It merges the structural precision and data-density of Linear with the fluid, reassuring polish of Stripe and Airbnb.

The visual style is **Contemporary Minimalist with Tonal Elevation**:
- **Trust & Integrity:** Clear visual status hierarchy, pristine hairline geometry, and unwavering structural alignment instill confidence across both informal trade networks and enterprise brokerages.
- **Warmth & Prestige:** Subtle undertones of warm alabaster and radiant Ethiopian amber counterbalance the clinical exactness of financial data, evoking institutional security and regional pride.
- **Tactile Clarity:** Deep visual distinction between active transactional flows, settled funds, and intermediary contracts using disciplined micro-states and crisp contrast.

## Colors

The palette leverages a bi-modal architecture engineered for legibility across variable field conditions and high-end desktop trading consoles.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (Emerald `#059669` / `#10B981`):** Represents verified escrow, collateral confirmation, successful settlement, and primary platform actions.
- **Secondary (Ethiopian Warm Gold/Amber `#D97706` / `#F59E0B`):** Denotes active custody, funds in transit, broker commissions, and pending verification stages.
- **Tertiary (Royal Cobalt `#2563EB`):** Applied strictly to auxiliary telemetry, legal contract links, and neutral broker-to-client handoffs.
- **Neutral (Slate `#0F172A` to `#F8FAFC`):** Grounds typography, high-contrast structural dividers, and data tables.

### Mode Architectures
- **Light Mode (Default):**
  - Base Surface: Warm Alabaster (`#FAFAFA`)
  - Elevated Container: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Key Text: Slate 900 (`#0F172A`)
  - Subtle Text: Slate 500 (`#64748B`)
  - Borders: Crisp hairline Slate 200 (`#E2E8F0`)
- **Dark Mode:**
  - Base Surface: Deep Obsidian (`#090D16`)
  - Elevated Container: Layered Slate (`#111827`)
  - Key Text: Slate 50 (`#F8FAFC`)
  - Subtle Text: Slate 400 (`#94A3B8`)
  - Borders: Subdued Slate 800 (`#1E293B`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, humanistic authority in display and section headers with **Inter** for disciplined numeric balance and dense transactional workflows.

- **Tabular Figures:** Always apply `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` to Inter for all currency representations (ETB/USD), transaction references, and escrow milestone trackers to prevent jitter during state updates.
- **Reading Rhythm:** High-density transaction logs use `body-md` paired with `label-sm` metadata. Marketing and broker onboarding use `display-hero` through `headline-lg` with tight negative letter tracking to retain modern executive presence.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system implements an adaptive 12-column layout balanced by an 8pt base grid for strict geometric predictability.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem), `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Single-column card stacks; transaction ledgers shift to responsive stacked cards.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin-tablet` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Master-detail split panels collapse to collapsible sheets.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, maximum canvas width of 1440px centered, `margin` (3rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Dual-pane layouts with persistent context bars for escrow balance and transaction status.
- **Spatial Rhythm:**
  - Layout containers use `space-xl` for sectional breathing room.
  - Form structures and transactional verification grids use strict `space-md` gaps.
  - Micro-pairings (icons with metrics, status chips) lock directly to `space-xs` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface stratification, structural borders, and diffuse ambient shadows rather than harsh drop shadows.

- **Surface Layering:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Warm Alabaster (`#FAFAFA`) in light mode; Deep Obsidian (`#090D16`) in dark mode.
  - **Level 1 (Default Containers & Tables):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border of Slate 200 (`#E2E8F0`). Dark mode: Slate 900 (`#111827`) with Slate 800 (`#1E293B`) border.
  - **Level 2 (Active Escrow Cards & Modals):** Layered White elevated by a subtle dual shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`. Dark mode utilizes a fine interior highlight border (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with an outer shadow of `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
- **Glassmorphic Overlays:** Sticky navigation bars and floating milestone progress bars employ `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a 90% opaque background surface to maintain context without obscuring ledger density.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is unified under Level 2 (`roundedness: 2`), delivering clean, contemporary corners that balance approachable consumer experience with structural financial enterprise standards.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Table Rows:** Base radius `0.5rem` (8px). Ensures precise alignment between adjacent controls and form inputs.
- **Cards, Drawers, and Section Modals:** `rounded-lg` at `1rem` (16px) creates self-contained workspaces for property listings, payment schedules, and broker credential sheets.
- **Status Pills, Verification Tags, and Avatars:** Fully circular/pill boundaries to instantly contrast operational data against rigid rectilinear layout blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Settlement/Execution):** Emerald background (`#059669`), White text, subtle `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`. On hover: `#047857`. Focus: ring of 2px Emerald with 2px offset.
- **Secondary (Escrow Action/Negotiate):** Slate 100 base in light mode (`#F1F5F9`), Slate 900 text. Dark mode: Slate 800 base (`#1E293B`), Slate 100 text.
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Transparent background with high-contrast text and a 1px Slate 200 border.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- **Container:** Height of 42px, 8px radius, Slate 200 border, Pure White fill.
- **State Changes:** On focus, active border transitions to Emerald (`#059669`) with an ambient outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`.
- **Currency/Numeric Inputs:** Fixed left-affix prefix ("ETB" or "USD") rendered in `label-md` Slate 400.

### Chips & Badges
- **Funded/Verified:** Emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`), Emerald text (`#065F46`), hairline border (`#A7F3D0`).
- **In Escrow/Pending Release:** Amber tint (`#FFFBEB`), Amber text (`#92400E`), hairline border (`#FDE68A`).
- **Disputed/Action Required:** Crimson tint (`#FEF2F2`), Crimson text (`#991B1B`), hairline border (`#FECACA`).

### Cards & Marketplace Items
- **Structure:** Crisp 1px border, 16px radius, internal padding of `space-lg`.
- **Header:** Accommodates split title, transaction ID in tabular font, and dynamic status chip.
- **Footer:** Separated by a hairline Slate 100/800 divider containing broker badge, milestone tracker, and primary execution button.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry:** 18px bounding box with 4px corner radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- **Active State:** Emerald fill (`#059669`) with pure white check/bullet icon.

### Specialized: Escrow Progress Stepper
- Horizontal chain of verification nodes: "Initiated", "Funds Deposited", "Inspection Complete", "Released".
- Completed steps display solid Emerald with checked glyphs; active intermediary steps display Amber with a glowing pulse ring; upcoming steps remain neutral hairline outlines.
